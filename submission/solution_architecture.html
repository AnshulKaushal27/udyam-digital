 PRODUCTION SPECIFICATION

1. System Overview & 4-Tier Cloud Architecture

Project Udyam Digital is a telco-grade, dual-portal digital onboarding platform for Indian MSMEs discovering, purchasing, and activating Airtel Business digital bundles:

  • Tier 1 — Multichannel Experience Layer: Dual independent frontend portals: Consumer Self-Serve Portal (/) and Airtel Control Command Centre (/control).
  • Tier 2 — Intelligence & Bundling Layer: Groq LLaMA 3.3 70B sales consultant, deterministic 5-factor scoring engine, 4 hero bundles, and 8-class quote recovery engine.
  • Tier 3 — Verification & Exception Layer: Smart KYC engine cross-validating GSTIN and Udyam against mock registries, OCR scoring, and 90-day utility bill date checks.
  • Tier 4 — Operations & Fulfillment Layer: Proactive Installation Risk Engine, bundle-specific SLA tracking, and unified exception action queue.

2. Dual Independent Portals Architecture

A. Consumer Portal (/): Lightweight, distraction-free interface built for non-technical MSME owners. Features consultative 1-question chat in Hinglish, transparent quotes with 18% GST breakdown, instant GSTIN/Udyam autofill, and consumer milestone stepper.

B. Airtel Control Portal (/control): Dark-slate enterprise command centre for Airtel employees and channel partners. Enforces strict backend RBAC across Super Administrator, Operations Manager, KYC Officer, Sales Executive, and Channel Partner.

3. AI-Powered Quote Recovery Engine (Phase 3)

Targets the 56.4% quote-stage collapse (1,113 drops in baseline). Employs an 8-class NLP objection classifier, priority scoring algorithm (0–100), automated down-tiering to entry bundles, personalized WhatsApp outreach drafts, and secure 1-click re-entry tokens (/api/recovery/resume/<token>).

4. Assisted Digital Partner Ecosystem & Commissions (Phase 4)

Enables local channel partners to conduct counter-side assisted selling via dedicated tablet mode. Features dynamic branded SVG QR codes with 30-day first-touch attribution lock and an activation-gated commission ledger releasing ₹1,500/activation strictly upon Step 5 service activation.

5. Smart KYC & Exception Resolution Engine (Phase 5)

Automates enterprise verification against mock_gstin_registry.csv (3,534 records; handles synthetic PAN 'X' rule) and mock_udyam_registry.csv (2,723 records). Features OCR quality scoring, deterministic 90-day utility bill freshness rule anchored to 15-Jul-2026, and actionable bilingual resolution guidance (English & Hindi).

6. Predictive Operations & Intelligence Centre (Phase 6)

Strict 4-Dimensional Metric Segregation: Separates Historical Baseline ($N=5,000$), Assignment Targets, Actual Prototype Results, and Simulated Scenarios with mandatory provenance tags.

6-Stage Funnel Diagnostics: Evaluates Stage 1 (Lead 5,000) through Stage 6 (Activated 368), isolating the 56.4% collapse point at Stage 3->4 with segment and city drilldowns.

Quote Loss Intelligence: Categorizes historical losses into Price Too High (414 / 37.2%), Competitor Cheaper (207 / 18.6%), Quote Complex (206 / 18.5%), and Delays (286 / 25.7%).

Bundle-Specific Proactive Risk Engine: Monitors milestones against bundle SLAs (Kirana 2d, Clinic 3d, Multi-site 7d, Mfg 14d) and scores risk (0-100) with explainable root causes.

Unified Exception Centre & What-If Simulator: Centralizes incident action queues (Acknowledge, Resolve) and interactive non-double-counting predictive modeling.
