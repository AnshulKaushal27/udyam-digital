- Live prototype transactions.
- Configured business targets.

Maintain consistent definitions.

Examples:

Overall conversion = Activated / Total Leads × 100.

Stage conversion = Next Stage Count / Previous Stage Count × 100.

Quote rejection rate = Rejected Quotes / Assessed Leads × 100, where the dataset definition supports that denominator.
